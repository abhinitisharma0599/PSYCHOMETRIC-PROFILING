# AI in Psychometric Profiling for Organisational Decision-Making | Systematic Review

## About This Project
This is a systematic research project on how artificial intelligence (AI) is used in psychometric profiling to support decision-making in organisations, especially in hiring and HR analytics.

## What I Did
- Reviewed 1,300+ academic records and combined insights from 52 selected studies using the PRISMA methodology.
- Studied AI techniques such as Natural Language Processing (NLP), behavioural analytics, and multimodal (video/audio) profiling.
- Evaluated these systems using psychometric standards: construct validity, reliability, and cross-context generalisability.
- Identified key challenges such as algorithmic bias, construct-proxy errors, and lack of transparency in AI-driven hiring tools.
- Developed a Human-Centred AI Framework to improve fairness, accountability, and decision-making in HR analytics.
- Showed the potential business impact: better hiring efficiency and lower bias risks.

## Methodology
- Systematic review following **PRISMA**
- 1,300+ records screened
- 52 studies selected for final analysis

## Key Topics Covered
- Artificial Intelligence in HR
- Psychometric profiling
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Behavioural analytics
- Multimodal (video/audio) profiling
- Construct validity and reliability
- Algorithmic bias and transparency
- Fairness and accountability

## Main Outcome
A **Human-Centred AI Framework** that helps organisations use AI in hiring and HR decisions in a fairer, more accountable, and more transparent way.
